## What is RapidEPR?

RapidEPR helps draft evaluation statements and award citations for the Army,
Air Force, Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard. Rapid Generator organizes drafts
into packages; RapidRegs helps research regulations with source links.

## How do credits work?

New accounts start with five credits. A bullet submission uses **one credit**,
including its generated options. An award submission uses **three credits**,
including AF1206. These are submission costs, not costs for copying each option.

You can buy credit packages or a personal Pro subscription in
[Settings → Credits](https://rapidepr.com/settings/credits). The current interface
lists Pro at $20/month; confirm the current price and terms at checkout.
RapidRegs currently has a separate request limit and does not debit these credits.

## How are unit and personal credits chosen?

If you belong to a unit with available credits or an active subscription,
generation uses that unit access first. If unit access cannot cover the
submission, it falls back to your personal subscription or credits.
See the [unit guide](/military-units/index).

## Where are my previous bullets and awards?

Open [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator). New work lives in its
package; the **History** view contains earlier standalone generations.
The old Bullet Generator and Award Generator links now lead to Rapid Generator.

## Can I trust a generated draft without checking it?

Review every fact, placeholder, name, date and formatting requirement.
Provide accurate scope and results in your input. Check the current form and
service guidance before submitting a draft. For RapidRegs, open the cited
publication and confirm that it applies to your situation.

## What if generation stops or my session expires?

Sign in again if asked. Reopen the package and look for a saved result before
submitting again; a connection can fail after saving. If your balance or result
is unclear, [contact support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support).

## How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage personal billing from [Settings → Credits](https://rapidepr.com/settings/credits).
Unit administrators manage unit billing in
[Settings → Units](https://rapidepr.com/settings/units). Ordinary subscription
cancellation is scheduled for the end of the billing period.

## What does deleting an account do?

The self-service account action cancels confirmed personal subscription billing
and disables sign-in. It retains stored account/content records; it is not a
data-erasure action. Account deletion does not cancel a unit's subscription
and does not automatically refund unused subscription time. If billing cannot
be safely confirmed, the action can fail and leave sign-in enabled.

For a data request or a partial failure, contact
[support@rapidroutesolutions.com](mailto:support@rapidroutesolutions.com).

## Are payment details stored?

Stripe handles payment-card collection. RapidEPR stores billing identifiers
and transaction metadata, such as purchase amounts and customer name/email.
Do not enter card details into accomplishment or support text fields.

## Can I belong to more than one unit?

The membership workflow supports one current unit. An administrator adds an
existing RapidEPR account by email. Leave the current unit before joining
another. The last unit administrator must arrange another administrator before
leaving. Personal credits stay with your account.

## How can I get help or suggest a feature?

Use [Settings → Support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support),
the [contact form](https://rapidepr.com/contact), or email
[support@rapidroutesolutions.com](mailto:support@rapidroutesolutions.com).
