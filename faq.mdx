## What is RapidEPR?

RapidEPR helps you write evaluation statements and award citations for the
Army, Air Force, Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard. RapidRegs answers
regulation questions with links to source publications.

## What is a package?

A package keeps drafts and favorites together for one person's evaluation
or award. Use separate packages for your own report, the people you supervise,
and award nominations you are preparing. Name each package for the person and
report period or award so you can find it later.

See [Rapid Generator](/rapid-generator) for instructions.

## How do credits work?

New accounts start with five credits. Each bullet submission uses one credit,
and each award submission uses three, including AF Form 1206. The cost covers
all options generated in that submission. Copying a draft does not use credits.

Buy credit packages in [Credits in Settings](https://rapidepr.com/settings/credits).
Pro costs $20 per month and includes unlimited bullet and award generation.

RapidRegs does not use these credits.

## Which credits does RapidEPR use first?

RapidEPR uses your unit's credits or subscription first. If the unit cannot
cover a submission, it uses your personal subscription or credits.

Read the [unit guide](/military-units/index) for more about shared credits.

## Where can I find my drafts?

Open [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator) and select the package
you were working on. The History tab contains standalone generations.

## How do I get a useful draft?

Include the person's actions, responsibilities and results. Use specific
numbers when you have them. Before submitting the report, check the draft's
facts and wording against the form and your rating chain's instructions.

For RapidRegs answers, open the cited publication and read the relevant paragraph.

## What if generation stops?

Reopen the package and look for a saved draft before generating again.
Sign in again if your session has expired. Contact
[support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support) if you need help with a draft
or credit charge.

## How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage your personal subscription in
[Credits in Settings](https://rapidepr.com/settings/credits).
Unit administrators manage unit subscriptions in
[Units in Settings](https://rapidepr.com/settings/units).

Cancellation stops renewal. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

## What happens when I delete my account?

RapidEPR cancels your personal subscription and disables sign-in.
It retains account and content records. Account deletion does not cancel
your unit's subscription or refund unused subscription time.

If account deletion fails, contact
[support@rapidroutesolutions.com](mailto:support@rapidroutesolutions.com).
Use the same address to request data deletion.

## Does RapidEPR store my payment details?

Stripe handles card details. RapidEPR stores billing identifiers, purchase
amounts and the customer's name and email.

## Can I belong to more than one unit?

You can belong to one unit at a time. A unit administrator adds you using
the email address of your RapidEPR account. Leave your unit before joining
another. Your personal credits stay with your account.

If you are the last administrator, promote another member before leaving.

## How can I get help or suggest a feature?

Open [Support in Settings](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support), use the
[contact form](https://rapidepr.com/contact), or email
[support@rapidroutesolutions.com](mailto:support@rapidroutesolutions.com).
