## Welcome to RapidEPR

RapidEPR helps you write military evaluation statements and award citations.
Use packages to keep drafts together for yourself or someone you supervise.
Use RapidRegs to look up regulations and read the sources behind an answer.

## Get started

1. [Sign in or create an account](https://rapidepr.com/sign-in).
2. Open Rapid Generator and choose New Package.
3. Choose your branch and a format under Bullets & Statements or Awards & Decorations.
4. Name the package for the person and report or award you are writing.
5. Enter their accomplishments and generate a draft.

Use Sign In with Google, or choose Create Account to register with an email
address and password. Complete any email verification the sign-up screen requests.

New accounts start with five credits. A bullet submission uses one credit,
and an award submission uses three. Each submission includes all the options
it generates.
