# Welcome to RapidEPR

RapidEPR helps you turn verified accomplishments into military evaluation
statements and award citations. You can organize drafts in named packages,
save favorites, and use RapidRegs to research regulations with source links.

## Get started

1. [Sign in or create an account](https://rapidepr.com/sign-in).
2. Open **Rapid Generator** and choose **New Package**.
3. Choose your branch, then a format under **Bullets & Statements** or **Awards & Decorations**.
4. Add the facts you want included, generate a draft, and review it before use.

New accounts start with five credits. A bullet generation uses one credit;
an award generation uses three. The cost is per submission, including the
options produced by that submission.
