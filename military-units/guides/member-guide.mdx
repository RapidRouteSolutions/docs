# Use your unit's shared access

Create a RapidEPR account, then ask your unit administrator to add its email
address. Open [Settings → Units](https://rapidepr.com/settings/units) to inspect
your current unit, balance and administrators.

You can belong to one current unit through the membership workflow. Leave
another unit before asking to be added.

## Generate as usual

Use [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator) to create statements or
award drafts. Available unit access is used first. If it cannot cover the
submission, the generator falls back to your personal subscription or credits.

A bullet submission costs one credit; an award submission costs three.
Administrators can inspect recorded usage statistics, not the text you generate.

## Leave or change a unit

Use **Leave Unit** in Settings → Units and confirm. Your access to its shared
pool ends, while personal credits and generated content remain with your account.
An administrator can add you again later.

If you are the last administrator, first promote another member. Contact an
existing administrator or [support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support)
when you need help changing membership.
