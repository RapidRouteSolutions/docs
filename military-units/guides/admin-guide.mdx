## Administer your unit

Open [Military Units in Settings](https://rapidepr.com/settings/units) to manage
members, billing and credit usage.

## Members and roles

Add a member using the email address of their RapidEPR account. They must
leave another unit before joining yours.

Use the member controls to remove members or change administrator roles.
Removing someone ends their access to the shared pool. Their personal
credits and generated content stay with their account.

If you are the last administrator, promote another member before leaving.

## Credits and subscriptions

| Option                 | Price                             |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 1,000 credits          | $250                              |
| 3,000 credits          | $600                              |
| Custom credits         | 15% discount, minimum 100 credits |
| Unlimited subscription | $750 per month                    |

Complete your purchase through Stripe. Purchased credits go into the shared
pool. Contact support if a completed purchase is missing from the balance.
Prices are in US dollars; checkout shows any applicable taxes.

RapidEPR uses unit credits or the unit subscription before members' personal
credits. Use the member usage statistics to plan future purchases.

## Cancel a subscription

Cancel renewal from the unit's billing controls. Members keep subscription
access until the end of the paid period. Deleting a member's personal account
does not cancel the unit subscription.

Contact [support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support) for help with
billing or membership.
