# Share credits with a unit

A unit gives your organization a shared credit pool or subscription.
Administrators manage membership and billing. Members write evaluations
and awards in their own packages.

## Request a unit

Open [Units in Settings](https://rapidepr.com/settings/units) and submit a
unit request. Once approved, you become its administrator and can add members
using the email addresses of their RapidEPR accounts.

Each person can belong to one unit at a time.

## Use shared credits

RapidEPR uses unit credits or the unit subscription first. If the unit cannot
cover a submission, it uses the member's personal subscription or credits.

A bullet submission costs one credit, and an award submission costs three.
Administrators can view member usage and purchase more credits.
They cannot view the text in members' packages.
