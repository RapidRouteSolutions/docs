# Share credits with a unit

Units let an organization share a credit pool or subscription. Administrators
manage members and billing; members generate statements and awards in their
own packages.

## Request a unit

Open [Settings → Units](https://rapidepr.com/settings/units), submit the unit
request, and wait for review. Approval makes the requester an administrator.
Then add members using the email addresses of their existing RapidEPR accounts.

The membership workflow supports one current unit per member. Members should
leave their current unit before being added to another.

## How shared access works

The generator attempts unit access before personal access. If the unit cannot
cover a submission, personal subscription access or credits can be used instead.
A bullet submission costs one credit; an award submission costs three.

Administrators can inspect recorded member usage and buy more credits.
Usage statistics do not grant access to a member's generated content.
