# Write an award citation

Create an award package for the person you are nominating. Keep their
accomplishments, drafts and favorite wording together while you prepare
the nomination.

Each award submission uses three credits, including AF Form 1206.
New accounts start with five credits.

## Generate a draft

1. Open [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator) and choose New Package.
2. Choose the person's branch, then a format under Awards & Decorations.
3. Name the package for the person and award.
4. Enter their rank, name, duty title, unit and award period.
5. Describe their accomplishments and results.
6. Generate a draft, review it and save the wording you want to use.

Citation formats require a rank, name, duty title and unit. Most produce a
complete citation with an opening, achievement narrative and closing. The Army
format produces three achievement narratives. AF Form 1206 organizes statements
into categories and does not require those member fields.

Spell out uncommon acronyms. Check the facts, dates and required citation
wording before submitting the award.
