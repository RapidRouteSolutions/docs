Welcome to the Award Generator! We're excited to help you create personalized military award citations with ease. Follow this guide to get started quickly.

**Note:** Each award generation uses **3 credits**.

## Create Your Account

To begin, sign in using your Google account or create a new account with your email address.

Once logged in, you'll receive **5 free credits** to explore the Award Generator's capabilities and see how it can simplify the process of generating military award citations. Your balance is shown on the **Credits** button in the top right corner of the app.

## Create a Package

Awards and bullets are written inside **packages**. A package keeps one member's work together, such as a single award package for someone you supervise.

1. Select **Rapid Generator** in the left sidebar, then select **New Package**.

2. **Branch:** choose your branch of service (Air Force, Army, Navy, Marines, or Coast Guard), then select **Continue**.

3. **Format:** under **Awards & Decorations**, choose the award format, such as **Achievement Medal** or **Commendation Medal** (the list depends on your branch), then select **Continue**.

4. **Create:** enter a **Package name**, such as the member's name and award, then select **Create Package**. You can rename it later.

## Generate Your First Award Citation

The package opens as soon as you create it.

1. In **Your accomplishment**, describe the individual's accomplishments (up to 500 characters).

2. Fill in the following details:

   - **Rank**: Enter the rank of the individual.
   - **Name**: Enter the name of the individual.
   - **Duty Title**: Enter the duty title of the individual.
   - **Unit**: Enter the unit of the individual.
   - **Time Period**: Choose the start and end dates of the award period.

3. Select **Generate Award**. Your citation appears under **Drafted Award**.

**Note:** Due to the increased complexity and increased word count, each award generation uses **3 credits**.

**Tip:** Avoid using uncommon acronyms as the AI might not recognize them. It's best to spell out terms explicitly, as awards generally do not contain acronyms. This will ensure the best results.

To keep a result handy, select its star (**Pin option**). Pinned results are listed in the package's **Pinned** section.

## Find Your Work Later

- **Packages:** select **Rapid Generator** in the left sidebar to see your packages. Use the search box or the **Active**, **Archived**, and **All** filters to find one, then open it to keep working.
- **Inside a package:** expand **Previous generations** for earlier citations and **Package activity** for a timeline of changes. Use **Edit Details** to rename the package or change its format; a format change applies to new generations only.
- **History:** the **History** tab on the Rapid Generator page lists your generated awards and bullets, including ones created before packages existed.
- **Archive and delete:** archiving makes a package read-only until you restore it. Deleting a package removes the package and its pins; the generated awards stay in **History**.

## Manage Your Credits

Need more credits? Select the **Credits** button at the top right of your screen, or go to **Settings** > **Credits**. There you can either:

- Purchase additional credits in packages
- Subscribe to our Pro plan for unlimited credits at $20/month
