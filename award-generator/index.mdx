# Generate an award draft

An award submission uses **three credits**, including AF Form 1206 submissions.
New accounts start with five credits.

1. Sign in and open [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator).
2. Choose **New Package**, select your branch, then a format under **Awards & Decorations**.
3. Name the package and open its editor.
4. Provide the member's rank, name, duty title, unit and award-period details,
   along with verified accomplishments.
5. Generate, review the completed draft, and save or copy your preferred wording.

Ordinary citation formats require rank, name, duty title and unit. **AF Form
1206** is an Air Force option with categorized output rather than a single
citation; its member fields are optional.

Spell out uncommon acronyms. Verify award eligibility, dates, opening/closing
language, form space and every factual statement before submission. Examples
and generated drafts do not replace the current award instructions.
