## Achievement Medal

| Field        | Example                   |
| ------------ | ------------------------- |
| Rank         | Petty Officer First Class |
| Name         | John Doe                  |
| Unit         | USCGC Example             |
| Duty title   | Operations Specialist     |
| Award period | 1 May to 31 December 2025 |

Accomplishments:

> Led 10 members through 20 search-and-rescue drills. Revised the watch
> handover checklist and reduced average response time from 12 minutes
> to eight.

Example citation:

> Petty Officer First Class John Doe is cited for superior performance of duty
> while serving as Operations Specialist for USCGC Example from 1 May 2025 to 31
> December 2025. Petty Officer Doe led 10 members through 20 search-and-rescue
> drills and revised the watch handover checklist, reducing average response
> time from 12 minutes to eight. His work prepared the team for faster handovers
> during search-and-rescue operations. By his initiative, professionalism, and
> devotion to duty, Petty Officer First Class Doe reflected great credit upon
> himself and upheld the highest traditions of the United States Coast Guard.
