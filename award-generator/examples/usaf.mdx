# Air Force award examples

Create an [award package](/rapid-generator) for the Airman you are nominating.
Choose the medal or AF Form 1206 format.

## Achievement Medal

| Field        | Example                                               |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Rank         | Staff Sergeant                                        |
| Name         | Jane Smith                                            |
| Unit         | 99th Communications Squadron                          |
| Duty title   | Noncommissioned Officer in Charge of Cyber Operations |
| Award period | 1 September to 31 December 2025                       |

Accomplishments:

> Trained 24 operators on revised incident-response procedures. Reduced
> the team's average response time from 40 minutes to 25.

Example citation:

> Staff Sergeant Jane Smith distinguished herself by outstanding achievement as
> Noncommissioned Officer in Charge of Cyber Operations, 99th Communications
> Squadron, from 1 September 2025 to 31 December 2025. During this period,
> Sergeant Smith trained 24 operators on revised incident-response procedures.
> Her instruction helped the team reduce its average response time from 40
> minutes to 25, allowing operators to begin addressing incidents 15 minutes
> sooner. The distinctive accomplishments of Sergeant Smith reflect credit upon
> herself and the United States Air Force.

## Commendation Medal

| Field        | Example                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Rank         | Technical Sergeant              |
| Name         | Michael Johnson                 |
| Unit         | 57th Maintenance Group          |
| Duty title   | Aircraft Maintenance Supervisor |
| Award period | 1 January to 31 December 2025   |

Accomplishments:

> Led 18 maintainers through 96 scheduled inspections. Revised the parts
> tracking process and reduced overdue maintenance actions from 27 to four.

Example citation:

> Technical Sergeant Michael Johnson distinguished himself by meritorious
> service as Aircraft Maintenance Supervisor, 57th Maintenance Group, from 1
> January 2025 to 31 December 2025. During this period, Sergeant Johnson led 18
> maintainers through 96 scheduled inspections. He revised the parts tracking
> process and reduced overdue maintenance actions from 27 to four, bringing the
> group's maintenance program closer to schedule. The distinctive
> accomplishments of Sergeant Johnson reflect credit upon himself and the United
> States Air Force.

## Meritorious Service Medal

| Field        | Example                            |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Rank         | Master Sergeant                    |
| Name         | Jane Doe                           |
| Unit         | 11th Security Forces Squadron      |
| Duty title   | Flight Chief                       |
| Award period | 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2025 |

Accomplishments:

> Supervised 42 defenders over three years. Qualified 16 new flight members
> and developed the security plan for an air show attended by 30,000 visitors.

Example citation:

> Master Sergeant Jane Doe distinguished herself in the performance of
> outstanding service to the United States as Flight Chief, 11th Security Forces
> Squadron, from 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2025. During this period, the
> outstanding professional skill, leadership, and ceaseless efforts of Sergeant
> Doe contributed to the squadron's security operations. She supervised 42
> defenders, qualified 16 new flight members, and developed the security plan
> for an air show attended by 30,000 visitors. The singularly distinctive
> accomplishments of Sergeant Doe reflect great credit upon herself and the
> United States Air Force.

## AF Form 1206

Use this format for statements grouped under Leadership and Job Performance
in Primary Duty and Whole Airman Concept.

Accomplishments:

> Led six maintainers in completing 24 overdue services and returning three
> vehicles to use. Organized a food drive that collected 850 pounds for a
> local food bank.

Example output:

Leadership and Job Performance in Primary Duty:

> [Rank/Name] led six maintainers through 24 overdue services, returning three
> vehicles to use.

Whole Airman Concept:

> [Rank/Name] organized a food drive that collected 850 pounds of food for a
> local food bank.
