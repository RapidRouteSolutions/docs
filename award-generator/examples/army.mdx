# Army award example

Choose Award Citation (Block 21) for an Army award. Create a
[package](/rapid-generator) for the Soldier and award you are preparing.

## Award Citation (Block 21)

This format returns three achievement narratives. Replace `[rank/name]`
with the Soldier's rank and name.

| Field        | Example                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Rank         | Sergeant First Class                  |
| Name         | John Doe                              |
| Unit         | 2nd Battalion, 123rd Example Regiment |
| Duty title   | Operations Sergeant                   |
| Award period | 1 January to 31 December 2025         |

Accomplishments:

> Built a training tracker used by four companies. Reduced weekly report
> preparation from six hours to two. Coordinated three field exercises
> involving 400 Soldiers.

Example output:

> [rank/name] built a training tracker for four companies, reducing weekly
> report preparation from six hours to two. This saved four hours of
> reporting work each week and gave the companies a shared way to track
> training requirements.

> [rank/name] reduced weekly report preparation from six hours to two by
> creating a training tracker for four companies. The tracker saved time
> on recurring reports and helped staff keep company training records
> organized.

> [rank/name] coordinated three field exercises involving 400 Soldiers.
> This work brought the participating companies into a common exercise
> schedule and supported the battalion's training program.
