> AND TRAINED THREE NEW TEAM LEADERS IN PATROL PLANNING AND COMMUNICATIONS
> PROCEDURES. HIS INSTRUCTION PREPARED THE TEAM LEADERS TO PLAN PATROLS AND
> DIRECT THEIR MARINES DURING THE EXERCISES. BY HIS INITIATIVE, PROFESSIONALISM,
> AND DEVOTION TO DUTY, SERGEANT DOE REFLECTED GREAT CREDIT UPON HIMSELF AND
> UPHELD THE HIGHEST TRADITIONS OF THE UNITED STATES NAVAL SERVICE.

## Meritorious Service Medal

| Field        | Example                                |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Rank         | Master Sergeant                        |
| Name         | Michael Johnson                        |
| Unit         | Marine Corps Forces Cyberspace Command |
| Duty title   | Cyber Operations Chief                 |
| Award period | 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2025     |

Accomplishments:

> Managed 30 Marines across three cyber operations teams. Built a training
> program that qualified 18 analysts and reduced the time to qualify from
> nine months to six.

Example citation:

> For outstanding meritorious service while serving as Cyber Operations Chief,
> Marine Corps Forces Cyberspace Command from 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2025. Master Sergeant Michael Johnson managed 30 Marines across three cyber
> operations teams and built a training program that qualified 18 analysts. The
> program reduced the time to qualify from nine months to six, preparing
> analysts for their team assignments three months sooner. By his leadership,
> technical expertise, and devotion to duty, Master Sergeant Johnson reflected
> great credit upon himself and upheld the highest traditions of the United
> States Naval Service.
