## Meritorious Service Medal

| Field        | Example                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Rank         | Senior Chief Petty Officer            |
| Name         | Michael Johnson                       |
| Unit         | Navy Cyber Defense Operations Command |
| Duty title   | Cyber Operations Senior Chief         |
| Award period | 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2025    |

Accomplishments:

> Managed 36 analysts across three watch teams. Established a qualification
> program that certified 20 new watchstanders and reduced unfilled watch
> positions from six to zero.

Example citation:

> For outstanding meritorious service while serving as Cyber Operations Senior
> Chief at Navy Cyber Defense Operations Command from 1 January 2023 to 31
> December 2025. Senior Chief Petty Officer Michael Johnson managed 36 analysts
> across three watch teams and established a qualification program that
> certified 20 new watchstanders. His efforts reduced unfilled watch positions
> from six to zero, providing each team with the qualified personnel it needed.
> By his leadership, technical expertise, and devotion to duty, Senior Chief
> Petty Officer Johnson reflected great credit upon himself and upheld the
> highest traditions of the United States Naval Service.
