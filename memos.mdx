<Note>
  Memo support is in development. This guide describes the planned workflow, not
  a feature that has shipped. The planned rollout is for all members, not a
  beta.
</Note>

## Choose a memo format

Memo is a third kind of writing in Rapid Generator, alongside Bullets &
Statements and Awards & Decorations. It is planned for the USAF branch
selection, including Guardians.

Choose one of four formats:

| Format                                | Use it for                                            |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Memorandum for record (MFR)           | Record facts, decisions or events.                    |
| Conversation or counseling            | Document a conversation or non-adverse counseling.    |
| Recommendation or character reference | Explain a recommendation with facts you can support.  |
| Appointment                           | Describe an appointment, duties and responsibilities. |

These formats do not cover letters of counseling (LOC), admonishment (LOA)
or reprimand (LOR), or other adverse actions.

<Warning>
  Do not upload or enter classified information. RapidEPR is not approved for
  controlled unclassified information (CUI). Avoid third-party personally
  identifiable information (PII), such as Social Security numbers, medical
  details or personal contact information. Check the entire uploaded file, not
  just the body you want to replace. These restrictions remain visible during
  upload and writing.
</Warning>

## Prepare your Word template

Upload a `.docx` template or example memo no larger than 5 MiB.
Legacy `.doc`, macro-enabled `.docm`, password-protected files and PDFs
are not supported.

For the clearest replacement boundaries, prepare your template in Word with:

- `{{MEMO_BODY}}` in a paragraph by itself where the new body belongs.
- `SUBJECT: {{MEMO_SUBJECT}}` where the new subject belongs.

An unambiguous standard memo layout with a SUBJECT line and an indented
signature block can also be detected automatically. RapidEPR replaces only
the subject and body; it does not edit arbitrary parts of a document.

Unsafe, corrupt, unsupported or ambiguous documents are rejected without
a charge. Follow the rejection guidance to fix the file. If the replacement
boundaries are unclear, use the explicit placeholders rather than asking
RapidEPR to guess.

An example supplies formatting, not facts for the new memo. Names, dates,
metrics and claims from its old body must not carry into the new body.

## Reuse a saved template

Upload once to save a template in your private member template library.
In another memo package, choose that saved template instead of uploading
the file again. Attaching an existing template is free.

The library belongs to you, not your unit; it is not a unit-shared library.
Saved templates are independent of the package where you first uploaded
them. Deleting that package does not delete the library template.

## Generate a Word memo

Once memo support is available:

1. Open [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator) and create a package.
2. Choose the USAF branch, Memo as the kind of writing, and a memo format.
3. Name the package so you can find it again.
4. Choose a saved template from your library, or upload a new Word template
   or example.
5. In the single instruction box, describe what happened and what the memo
   should say. Supply the facts needed for the conversation, recommendation,
   appointment or record.
6. Generate for 3 Credits, then preview the completed Word version.

Each successful generation produces one saved Word version. Your attached
template is available when you return to the package and can also be reused
across packages through your library. Templates and generated versions are
private to you.

Missing facts should appear as placeholders, not invented details. Check
the subject, body, names, dates and placeholders. Generated wording is not
proof that an event occurred or an appointment was authorized.

## Review and download

Preview the generated Word document, then download the editable `.docx`.
Preview and download are free for every saved Word version. The browser
preview renders the Word file, but its pagination may differ from desktop
Word.

Your uploaded heading, signer and other non-body content remain as uploaded,
along with the template's images, styles and page settings. Review all of
that retained content: an old date, routing detail or signer is not updated
automatically. Complete any remaining changes, routing and signing in Word.

RapidEPR does not provide PDF upload or export, arbitrary document editing,
or a guarantee of official correspondence or legal compliance. Check your
unit's requirements before using the document.

## Revise from a saved version

Choose a previous saved version as the basis for a revision, then enter
your instruction, such as “make it more concise.” A revision can use that
version's content as context; supply any new facts in your instruction.
Each AI revision costs 3 Credits.

Saved versions are immutable: each retains its own template and Word output.
A revision creates a new version rather than overwriting the old one. Each
remains available to preview and download for free. Attaching a different
template does not change earlier downloads or their original layouts.

You can also download a version and edit it yourself in Word without spending
more RapidEPR Credits.

Older text-only memo history is retained, but a version created without a
Word template has no Word file to download.

## Credits and unit funding

One successful memo generation or AI revision costs 3 Credits, the same as
an award generation. Upload, validation, attaching a saved template, preview
and download are free.
Rejected requests, refusals and export or rendering failures do not consume
Credits; you do not pay for a version that cannot be downloaded.

Memo generation follows the same funding rules as Awards: RapidEPR uses
your unit's credits or subscription first. If the unit cannot cover the
submission, it uses your personal subscription or credits.
See the [Military Units member guide](/military-units/guides/member-guide)
for membership and funding details.
