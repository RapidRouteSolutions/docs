<Note>
Memo support is in development. This guide describes the planned workflow,
not a feature that has shipped. The planned rollout is for all members, not
a beta.
</Note>

## Choose a memo format

Memo is a third kind of writing in Rapid Generator, alongside Bullets &
Statements and Awards & Decorations. It is planned for the USAF branch
selection, including Guardians.

Choose one of four formats:

| Format | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Memorandum for record (MFR) | Record facts, decisions or events. |
| Conversation or counseling | Document a conversation or non-adverse counseling. |
| Recommendation or character reference | Explain a recommendation with facts you can support. |
| Appointment | Describe an appointment, duties and responsibilities. |

These formats do not cover letters of counseling (LOC), admonishment (LOA)
or reprimand (LOR), or other adverse actions.

<Warning>
Do not enter classified information. RapidEPR is not approved for controlled
unclassified information (CUI). Avoid third-party personally identifiable
information (PII), such as Social Security numbers, medical details or
personal contact information. This warning remains visible while you work
on a memo.
</Warning>

## Prepare a draft

Once memo support is available:

1. Open [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator) and create a package.
2. Choose the USAF branch, Memo as the kind of writing, and a memo format.
3. Name the package so you can find it again.
4. Complete the guided fields for your format. Supply the purpose, relevant
   facts and any requested details about the conversation, recommendation
   or appointment. Use only information you are allowed to enter.
5. Generate the memo and review its subject and body.

Each generation produces one draft, not several alternatives. Check names,
dates, facts and any placeholders before using it. Do not treat generated
wording as proof that an event occurred or an appointment was authorized.

## Credits and unit funding

One memo generation costs 3 Credits, the same as an award generation.
Regenerating costs another 3 Credits, even if you only change a few inputs.

Memo generation follows the same funding rules as Awards: RapidEPR uses
your unit's credits or subscription first. If the unit cannot cover the
submission, it uses your personal subscription or credits.
See the [Military Units member guide](/military-units/guides/member-guide)
for membership and funding details.

## Edit and copy

Editing the draft is free. Revise the wording yourself rather than
regenerating when you only need a small change.

Add, remove or reorder paragraphs, or indent and outdent them. Numbering
updates automatically through `1.`, `a.`, `(1)`, `(a)`. A single top-level
paragraph is unnumbered. Review the paragraph levels before copying.

Copy the body separately from the subject. Paste each into the appropriate
place in your unit's memo template.

## Finish in your unit's template

RapidEPR produces a subject and body only. It does not produce a PDF,
letterhead or a finished, signed memorandum.

Add the subject and body to your approved template, then complete the
letterhead, routing information, date and signature block there. Check your
unit's formatting and review requirements before routing or signing.
