# Look up regulations

Open [Rapid Regs](https://rapidepr.com/regs) and ask your question. Name the
service or publication when you can. Auto selects a service from your question,
or you can choose the service yourself.

For example, ask "What does AR 670-1 say about wearing headphones in uniform?"
rather than "Can I wear headphones?"

Each question can contain up to 1,500 characters. Ask follow-up questions in
the same chat to continue the discussion.

## Read the sources

Use the source links below an answer to open the publications. Check the
relevant paragraph, effective date and whether the rule applies to your situation.

Source match scores measure how closely a publication matches your question.
They do not measure the accuracy of the answer.

If an answer has a citation warning, ask for the exact publication or paragraph
and check the source before relying on it.

## Saved chats

Your chats appear in the sidebar so you can return to them. If a chat fails to
save, RapidRegs displays a warning.

RapidRegs does not use bullet or award credits. If you reach the request limit,
wait before sending another question.

Deleting a chat removes it from your history. RapidEPR retains the messages.
Contact [support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support) with a data deletion request.
