# Research regulations

Open [Rapid Regs](https://rapidepr.com/regs) from the sidebar after sign-in.
Ask a specific question and name the service, publication or topic when you
can. Auto selection can infer a service; choose it explicitly if the question
is ambiguous.

Questions can contain up to **1,500 characters**. Ask follow-up questions in
the same chat when you want to keep the context. RapidRegs uses recent
conversation context, not an unlimited memory of every earlier turn.

## Review the sources

Answers can include source chips linking to retrieved publications. Open the
source and check the relevant paragraph, effective date and applicability.
A displayed retrieval score describes how closely a source matched the query;
it is not a confidence percentage for the answer.

A citation warning means sources were missing, lacked usable links or did not
match a reference in the answer. Rephrase or ask for the exact publication,
and verify it independently before acting. Corpus coverage and publication
availability can vary.

## History and limits

The chat saves to your account when storage succeeds. A warning may tell you
that an answer could not be saved. A new chat title may update shortly after
the answer completes.

RapidRegs currently uses a separate request limit rather than deducting bullet
or award credits. If you reach the rate limit, wait before trying again.

Deleting a chat hides it from your history; it does not erase retained records
or provider-side data. For data-handling questions, contact
[support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support).
