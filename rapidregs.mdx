## Look up regulations

Open [Rapid Regs](https://rapidepr.com/regs) and ask your question. Name the
service or publication when you can. Auto selects a service from your question,
or you can choose the service yourself.

For example, ask "What does AR 670-1 say about wearing headphones in uniform?"
rather than "Can I wear headphones?"

Each question can contain up to 1,500 characters. Ask follow-up questions in
the same chat to continue the discussion. Choose New chat to start another topic.

## Example question and answer

This example was captured in RapidRegs on October 17, 2026 with Army selected.

Question:

> In two sentences, what is AR 670-1 used for? Cite the official publication.

Answer:

> **Bottom line:** AR 670-1, _Wear and Appearance of Army Uniforms and Insignia_, establishes Army policy for **proper uniform wear and appearance**, including insignia [AR 670-1]. Soldiers and commanders use it to determine authorized standards and **enforce uniform and grooming compliance** [AR 670-1].

The answer linked to [AR 670-1 — Wear and Appearance of Army Uniforms and Insignia](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN30302-AR_670-1-001-WEB-3.pdf).
Use the source link to check the publication yourself. Answers and retrieved
sources can change between runs.

## Read the sources

Use the source links below an answer to open the publications. Check the
relevant paragraph, effective date and whether the rule applies to your situation.

The source link may show a score labeled Confidence, such as 94/100 in this
example. This is a source match score: it measures how closely a publication
matches your question, not the accuracy of the answer.

If an answer has a citation warning, ask for the exact publication or paragraph
and check the source before relying on it.

## Saved chats

Your chats appear in the sidebar so you can return to them. If a chat fails to
save, RapidRegs displays a warning.

RapidRegs does not use bullet or award credits. If you reach the request limit,
wait before sending another question.

Deleting a chat removes it from your history. RapidEPR retains the messages.
You cannot restore a deleted chat from the interface.
Contact [support](https://rapidepr.com/settings/support) with a data deletion request.
