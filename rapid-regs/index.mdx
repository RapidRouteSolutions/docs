title: Getting Started
---

Rapid Regs answers questions about military regulations, policies, and standards, and lists the source publications behind each answer so you can check them yourself.

## Ask Your First Question

1. Sign in, then select **Rapid Regs** in the left sidebar.

2. Choose a branch from the branch menu (**Air Force**, **Army**, **Navy**, **Marine Corps**, or **Coast Guard**), or leave it on **Auto detect** to let Rapid Regs work it out from your question.

3. Type your question in the box at the bottom of the page, such as "What does AR 670-1 say about earbuds in uniform?", and press Enter or select the send button. You can also start with one of the suggested questions.

4. Read the answer and open the publications listed under **Sources** to confirm the wording.

**Tip:** Name the publication, chapter, or paragraph when you know it. Specific questions get more precise answers.

## Check the Sources

Rapid Regs is a research aid, not an official publication. Always verify an answer against the official publication before acting on it.

If Rapid Regs can't match every publication it named, can't attach a link, or didn't find source material for an answer, it shows a warning on that answer. Treat those answers with extra caution.

## Return to Earlier Chats

Your chats are saved to **History**. On wider screens, the History panel sits beside the chat; on a phone, open it with the history button at the top of the page. Select a saved chat to pick up where you left off, or start fresh with **New chat**. Changing the branch on a chat that already has messages also starts a new chat.

To remove a saved chat, select the delete button next to it in History and confirm. Deleted chats can't be recovered.
