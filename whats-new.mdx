Select **What's new** near the bottom of the dashboard sidebar to browse product
Announcements. If your sidebar is collapsed, use the What's new icon. On mobile,
open the navigation menu first.

The feed opens in a drawer on the right, leaving your current page in place.
Announcements appear newest first, with a publication date, short description and
an optional action to try the capability. Use the close button or press Escape to
return to your work.

## What the badge means

The badge counts Announcements you have not yet acknowledged. Opening the drawer
acknowledges the Announcements available at that opening, including cards below the
fold. You do not need to click each card or scroll through the entire feed.

Your acknowledgement is saved across browsers and devices, separately from other
Members in your Unit. Previously acknowledged Announcements remain available to
browse, and a newly published Announcement can add a fresh badge.

New Members can browse older Announcements without inheriting an unread backlog.
Copy corrections do not make an acknowledged Announcement unread again.

<Note>
  If saving your acknowledgement fails, you can still browse and close the
  drawer. The badge stays unread until saving succeeds; opening the drawer again
  can retry.
</Note>

## Explore Rapid Regs

Rapid Regs discovery now lives in the **Explore Rapid Regs** Announcement rather
than a separate **Updated** pill in navigation. Select its action to open Rapid
Regs. Always verify official regulation sources before acting on an answer.

What's new is a curated feed of meaningful capabilities, not a notification
center for billing alerts, tasks or outages.
