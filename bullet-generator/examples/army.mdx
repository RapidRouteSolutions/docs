# Army evaluation examples

Choose the format for the Soldier's grade and the section you are writing.
Keep their drafts in a [package](/rapid-generator) named for the Soldier
and rating period.

## NCOER (SGT)

Use this format for SGT evaluations. Select Character, Presence, Intellect,
Leads, Develops, Achieves or Overall Performance Comments.

Input:

> Led a four-Soldier team through 18 training events and certified three Soldiers.

Draft for Leads:

> o led four Soldiers through 18 training events; certified three Soldiers for team duties

## NCOER (SSG-1SG)

Use this format for SSG, SFC, MSG and 1SG evaluations. Select the rated rank
and report section.

Input:

> Managed the platoon maintenance tracker, trained 12 Soldiers and closed 27
> overdue services. Equipment readiness rose from 82% to 94%.

Draft for Achieves:

> o closed 27 overdue services and trained 12 Soldiers on the maintenance tracker;
> raised equipment readiness from 82% to 94%

## NCOER (CSM/SGM)

Use this format for CSM and SGM evaluations. Sections include Character,
Attributes and Competencies Narrative, and Overall Performance Comments.

Input:

> Advised the battalion command team during deployment, coordinated six
> companies and mentored 18 senior NCOs. Sponsorship completion rose from
> 70% to 96%.

Draft for Attributes and Competencies Narrative:

> Advised the battalion command team across six deployed companies and mentored
> 18 senior NCOs. Coordinated sponsorship efforts that raised completion from
> 70% to 96%.

## NCOER

Use this format for a standalone NCOER bullet.

Input:

> Created a training tracker and helped supervisors close 35 overdue
> requirements for 20 Soldiers.

Draft:

> o created a training tracker for 20 Soldiers; helped supervisors close
> 35 overdue requirements

## NCOER - Tailored (Legacy)

Select an area of performance to guide the bullet.

Input:

> Led a nine-Soldier squad through four field exercises and coached two
> new team leaders.

Draft for Leads:

> o led nine Soldiers through four field exercises; coached two new team
> leaders on squad planning and execution

## OER (Company Grade)

Use this format for DA Form 67-10-1, covering 2LT through CPT. Choose the
section you need, such as Leads, Achieves or Senior Rater Comments on Potential.

Input:

> Served as company XO during an NTC rotation, coordinated 14 logistics
> packages and trained six new lieutenants.

Draft for Leads:

> Coordinated 14 logistics packages during the company's NTC rotation and
> trained six new lieutenants in planning and support procedures.

## OER (Field Grade)

Use this format for DA Form 67-10-2, covering MAJ and LTC. Sections include
Performance Narrative and assignment recommendations.

Input:

> Served as battalion S3 during a JRTC rotation. Synchronized five maneuver
> companies through 12 missions.

Draft for Performance Narrative:

> Synchronized five maneuver companies through 12 missions as battalion S3
> during the JRTC rotation. Coordinated company plans and the battalion's
> operating schedule throughout the exercise.

## OER (Strategic / Colonel)

Use this format for DA Form 67-10-3. Include the scope of command,
partnerships and results.

Input:

> Commanded a brigade through a CTC rotation with joint and multinational
> forces. Established a leader development program for 24 field-grade officers.

Draft for Performance and Potential Narrative:

> Commanded the brigade through a CTC rotation with joint and multinational
> forces. Established a leader development program for 24 field-grade officers.

## OER

Use this format for a standalone OER narrative.

Input:

> Built a reporting tracker that reduced average staff reporting delays
> from three days to one across six sections.

Draft:

> Built a tracker for six staff sections that reduced average reporting delays
> from three days to one, giving commanders earlier access to staff reports.

## Senior Rater Comment

Include the comparison group, ranking and recommendations you can support.

Input:

> Top 10% of the 24 captains I senior rate. Ready for company command and
> resident PME.

Draft:

> Top 10% of the 24 captains I senior rate. Select for company command and
> resident PME.

## Award Achievement

Use this format for an Army award achievement narrative. Include the action
and its result.

Input:

> Built a training tracker for 80 Soldiers and cleared 46 overdue requirements.

Draft:

> Built a training tracker for 80 Soldiers and cleared 46 overdue requirements,
> giving leaders an accurate view of the unit's remaining training needs.
