# Navy evaluation examples

Choose the format for the Sailor's report and grade. Keep each person's
drafts in a separate [package](/rapid-generator).

## EVAL - Tailored (E1-E6)

Use this format for NAVPERS 1616/26. Select a trait, Career Recommendations,
Comments on Performance or Qualifications / Achievements.

Input:

> E6 Jones led 14 Sailors through INSURV preparation. Corrected 184 discrepancies,
> raised maintenance completion from 72% to 96% and qualified nine Sailors.

Draft excerpt for Comments on Performance:

> Led 14 Sailors through INSURV preparation, correcting 184 discrepancies and
> raising maintenance completion from 72% to 96%. Qualified nine Sailors
> before the inspection.

## FITREP - Tailored (W2-O6)

Use this format for NAVPERS 1610/2. Select the section you are writing,
such as duties, a trait, career recommendations or comments on performance.

Input:

> LT Smith served as communications officer for 12 months, led 18 Sailors
> and managed a $2.4 million radio suite. Also served as CMS custodian.

Draft excerpt for duties:

> COMMUNICATIONS OFFICER-12; CMS CUSTODIAN-12. Led 18 Sailors and managed
> a $2.4 million radio suite.

## Chief Eval

Use this format for comments grouped under the seven CHIEFEVAL traits:
Technical Mastery, Institutional Expertise, Professionalism, Integrity,
Accountability, Deckplate Leadership and Team Effectiveness.

Input:

> Led the Chief's Mess through a seven-month deployment. Mentored 12
> first-class petty officers and helped eight qualify as new watchstanders.

Draft for Deckplate Leadership:

> Mentored 12 first-class petty officers through a seven-month deployment,
> helping eight qualify as new watchstanders.

You can copy comments from each trait group separately.

## Chief Eval Summary (Block 40)

Use this format for the Reporting Senior Comments on Performance section
of NAVPERS 1616/27.

Input:

> Led the Chief's Mess through deployment, mentored five first-class petty
> officers who advanced and sustained 95% retention.

Draft excerpt:

> Led the Chief's Mess through deployment and mentored five first-class petty
> officers who advanced. Sustained 95% retention during the reporting period.

## Eval Bullet

Use this format for a standalone enlisted EVAL statement.

Input:

> Built a maintenance tracker for three work centers and cut weekly
> report preparation from four hours to one.

Draft:

> Built a maintenance tracker for three work centers, cutting weekly report
> preparation from four hours to one and returning three hours to maintenance work.

## Opening Statement

Provide the performance or ranking you want to introduce.

Input:

> Top E5 in the work center. Led INSURV preparation and helped six Sailors qualify.

Draft:

> TOP E5 IN THE WORK CENTER. LED INSURV PREPARATION AND HELPED SIX SAILORS QUALIFY.

## Closing Statement

Provide your advancement and assignment recommendations.

Input:

> Recommended for advancement to E6 and assignment as an LPO or instructor.

Draft:

> RECOMMENDED FOR ADVANCEMENT TO E6 AND ASSIGNMENT AS AN LPO OR INSTRUCTOR.
