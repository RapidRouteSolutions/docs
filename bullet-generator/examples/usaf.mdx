## Air Force evaluation examples

Use a [package](/rapid-generator) for each Airman's evaluation. Choose
OPB Section or EPB Section when writing a specific part of the report.

These examples were generated in RapidEPR on October 17, 2026 with fictional
inputs and Moderate variation. Each example shows one complete returned option. Review added claims and replace
placeholders such as `xx` with verified facts before use.

## OPB Section (Officer)

Choose the section you need on the officer's OPB, such as Duty Description,
Executing the Mission, Leading People or HLR Performance Statement.

| Setting        | Selection             |
| -------------- | --------------------- |
| Variation      | Moderate              |
| Area / Section | Executing the Mission |

Input:

> Commanded A-Flight's 38 Airmen and managed $14 million in flightline support equipment. Raised sortie recovery from 88% to 96% across 42 missions.

Output (Option 1):

> Commanded 38 A-Flight Airmen and applied $14M in flightline support equipment to raise sortie recovery from 88% to 96% across 42 missions, strengthening mission execution and improving the flight’s ability to recover sorties on schedule.
