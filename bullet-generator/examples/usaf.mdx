# Air Force evaluation examples

Use a [package](/rapid-generator) for each Airman's evaluation. Choose
OPB Section or EPB Section when writing a specific part of the report.

## OPB Section (Officer)

Choose the section you need on the officer's OPB, such as Duty Description,
Executing the Mission, Leading People or HLR Performance Statement.

Input:

> Commanded A-Flight's 38 Airmen and managed $14 million in flightline
> support equipment. Raised sortie recovery from 88% to 96% across 42 missions.

Draft for Executing the Mission:

> Led 38 Airmen and managed $14 million in flightline equipment across 42 missions,
> raising sortie recovery from 88% to 96%.

## EPB Section (Enlisted)

Choose the EPB section and the Airman's rated grade.

Input:

> As a SSgt and A-Flight lead trainer, trained 10 Airmen through 43 core tasks.

Draft for Leading People:

> Trained 10 Airmen through 43 core tasks as A-Flight lead trainer, expanding
> the flight's pool of personnel qualified to perform those tasks.

## EPB/OPB

Use this format for a standalone performance statement.

Input:

> Led five maintainers on night shift to return two aircraft to service
> before their scheduled missions.

Draft:

> Led five maintainers in returning two aircraft to service during night shift,
> meeting the next day's mission schedule.

## EPB/OPB - Tailored (Legacy)

Choose one of the four Major Performance Areas to guide the statement.

Input:

> Created a tracker for 52 overdue training requirements. Helped supervisors
> clear the backlog in six weeks.

Draft for Improving the Unit:

> Built a tracker for 52 overdue training requirements and helped supervisors
> clear the backlog in six weeks.

## ALQ

Name the Airman Leadership Quality you want to address and describe the
supporting action.

Input:

> Communication. Briefed 18 maintainers on a new aircraft inspection process
> and answered their questions before the first shift used it.

Draft:

> Briefed 18 maintainers on a new aircraft inspection process and resolved
> questions before the first shift put it into use.

## Duty Description

Describe the job, mission and scope of responsibility. Include personnel,
equipment or budget figures that belong to the role.

Input:

> Assistant director of operations for a flying squadron with 75 aircrew
> and 25 aircraft. Coordinates daily flying operations and training.

Draft:

> Coordinates daily flying operations and training as Assistant Director of
> Operations for a squadron with 75 aircrew and 25 aircraft.

## HLR Assessment

Provide the performance you want the higher level reviewer to address.
Use the OPB or EPB section formats for separate stratification fields
and mandatory comments.

Input:

> Led inspection preparation, corrected 64 findings and raised program
> compliance from 78% to 96%.

Draft:

> Led inspection preparation that corrected 64 findings and raised program
> compliance from 78% to 96%.
