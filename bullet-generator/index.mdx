# Generate evaluation statements

A bullet submission uses **one credit**, including all options returned for
that submission. New accounts start with five credits.

1. Sign in and open [Rapid Generator](https://rapidepr.com/generator).
2. Choose **New Package**, select your branch, then a format under **Bullets & Statements**.
3. Name the package and open its editor.
4. Describe what you did, the scope of responsibility, and the verified result.
5. Choose the section/area and rated rank when the format requests them.
6. Choose a variation if offered, then generate.
7. Review the completed options, save favorites and copy the wording you need.

For form-specific work, start with the available section-aware option:
Air Force OPB/EPB sections, Army grade-specific NCOER/OER formats, or Navy
EVAL/FITREP formats. General and legacy formats remain available.

Use only facts you can support. Replace placeholders with accurate details;
do not adopt invented quantities. Check the actual form's available space,
current service guidance and your rating chain's requirements. A generated
draft is not a guarantee that every limit or requirement has been met.
