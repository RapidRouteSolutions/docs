Welcome to RapidEPR! We're excited to help you streamline your military performance report writing process. Here's a quick guide to get you up and running in no time.

**Note:** Each bullet generation uses **1 credit**.

## Create Your Account

To get started, sign in using your Google account or create a new account with your email address.

Once logged in, you'll receive **5 free credits** so you can explore RapidEPR's capabilities and see how it can simplify military performance reports for you. Your balance is shown on the **Credits** button in the top right corner of the app, so you can easily track how many you have remaining.

## Create a Package

Bullets and awards are written inside **packages**. A package keeps one member's work together: yourself, someone you supervise, or a specific rating period.

1. Select **Rapid Generator** in the left sidebar, then select **New Package**.

2. **Branch:** choose your branch of service (Air Force, Army, Navy, Marines, or Coast Guard), then select **Continue**.

3. **Format:** under **Bullets & Statements**, choose the format you need, then select **Continue**. For the most accurate output, pick a form-aware option when available, such as Air Force **OPB Section (Officer)** or **EPB Section (Enlisted)**, Army grade-specific **NCOER** / **OER** options, or Navy **EVAL - Tailored (E1-E6)** / **FITREP - Tailored (W2-O6)**.

4. **Create:** enter a **Package name**, such as the member's name or rating period, then select **Create Package**. You can rename it later.

## Generate Your First Bullet

The package opens as soon as you create it.

1. In **Your accomplishment**, describe what was done in plain English (up to 500 characters). No need to worry about military formatting - RapidEPR will take care of that for you!

2. If the format asks for an **Area / Section** or **Rated Rank**, choose the one that matches where you plan to paste the output. This helps RapidEPR match the right tone, character range, and formatting.

3. Choose a **Variation**. Conservative stays closest to the facts you provided, Moderate balances polish and restraint, and Creative gives RapidEPR more room to vary the wording while still using placeholders for missing details.

4. Select **Generate Bullets**. Your options appear under **Drafted Bullets**.

To keep an option handy, select its star (**Pin option**). Pinned options are listed in the package's **Pinned** section.

## Find Your Work Later

- **Packages:** select **Rapid Generator** in the left sidebar to see your packages. Use the search box or the **Active**, **Archived**, and **All** filters to find one, then open it to keep working.
- **Inside a package:** expand **Previous generations** for earlier output and **Package activity** for a timeline of changes. Use **Edit Details** to rename the package or change its format; a format change applies to new generations only.
- **History:** the **History** tab on the Rapid Generator page lists your generated bullets and awards, including ones created before packages existed.
- **Archive and delete:** archiving makes a package read-only until you restore it. Deleting a package removes the package and its pins; the generated bullets stay in **History**.

## Manage Your Credits

Need more credits? Select the **Credits** button at the top right of your screen, or go to **Settings** > **Credits**. There you can either:

- Purchase additional credits in packages
- Subscribe to our Pro plan for unlimited credits at $20/month
