Updates to help you write evaluations, prepare awards and research regulations.
The newest releases are listed first.

## October 2026

### Clearer drafts and grade-specific EPB sections

Chief Eval results now show readable trait names and identify traits that need
more supporting accomplishments. Chief Eval and AF Form 1206 drafts stay
readable in sections throughout generation.

The EPB section picker now limits MSgt-SMSgt Stratification to those grades and
CMSgt Higher Responsibility Support to CMSgt. Select the rated Airman's grade
to see the sections that apply.

[See Navy evaluation examples](/bullet-generator/examples/navy) or
[Air Force evaluation examples](/bullet-generator/examples/usaf).

### Better source handling in RapidRegs

Source links now prioritize publications cited in the answer. Publications marked
as retired are filtered out, and citation checks better recognize renamed
publications and revision numbers, reducing unnecessary citation warnings.
Stray formatting characters have also been removed from answers.

[Look up a regulation with RapidRegs](/rapidregs).

### Find packages and start writing more easily

Package search now checks beyond the first loaded page, so you can find older
work without loading each page yourself. Use the Active, Archived or All filter
to choose which packages to search.

Get started links for writing tools now take you through sign-in and into the
new-package workflow. Unit approval emails open the current Military Units
settings page.

[Open the package guide](/rapid-generator).

### Drafting and subscription fixes

Fixed cases where routine military accomplishments received unnecessary refusal
messages. Air Force award drafts now use the Air and Space
Achievement Medal and Air and Space Commendation Medal names.

Subscription fixes address cases where access reflected an older subscription
after a plan change. Subscription status is also refreshed regularly to keep
access aligned with billing.

### More writing guides

New guides cover common evaluation and award-writing tasks:

- [Senior NCO EPB examples for MSgt and SMSgt](https://rapidepr.com/blog/senior-nco-epb-examples-msgt-and-smsgt-performance-statements)
- [NCOER bullets for 91-series mechanics and maintainers](https://rapidepr.com/blog/ncoer-bullets-for-91-series-mechanics-and-maintainers)
- [Achievement Medal citation examples for Army and Air Force](https://rapidepr.com/blog/achievement-medal-citation-examples-army-air-force)

## August 2026

### Packages for evaluations and awards

Rapid Generator brings bullet and award writing into one workspace. Create a
named package for yourself, someone you supervise, or a specific report period.
Keep the person's drafts together, pin useful options, and archive the package
when the work is finished.

You can switch formats within the package's branch and type of writing. Earlier
generations keep their original format. The refreshed navigation also puts
account controls in the sidebar and supports light, dark and system themes.

[Learn how to work in a package](/rapid-generator).

### Formatting checks and more useful history

Generation checks the draft's structure and supported length limits before it
finishes. A draft can restart once while RapidEPR tries to correct a formatting
problem. Review the completed result before using it.

Previous generations now show the original input alongside the output, making
it easier to compare drafts and see which accomplishment produced each result.
History also displays readable format and section names.

## May 2026

### RapidRegs chat with linked sources

Research regulations for the Army, Air Force, Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard
in a streaming chat. Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation, open the
linked publications, and return to saved chats with automatically generated
titles.

RapidRegs also gained support for available joint publications and Department
of Defense-wide guidance alongside service publications.

[Read the RapidRegs guide](/rapidregs).

### Searchable writing guides

The [RapidEPR blog](https://rapidepr.com/blog) provides evaluation and award
writing guides with practical examples. Search the articles or browse by page
to find help for your branch and task.

## April 2026

### Air Force OPB and EPB sections

OPB Section lets officers draft individual report sections, including
Duty Description, Rater Stratification, the four Major Performance Areas,
Mandatory Comments and HLR sections.

EPB Section lets enlisted Airmen choose a report section and rated grade.
Sections include Duty Description, the four Major Performance Areas,
Mandatory Comments, MSgt-SMSgt Stratification, Future Roles and HLR Comments.
The [current Air Force guide](/bullet-generator/examples/usaf) also covers
grade-specific section availability.

[See Air Force examples](/bullet-generator/examples/usaf).

### Army NCOER and OER formats by grade

Choose the format for the rated Soldier's grade:

| Format                    | Grades                    |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| NCOER (SGT)               | SGT                       |
| NCOER (SSG-1SG)           | SSG, SFC, MSG and 1SG     |
| NCOER (CSM/SGM)           | CSM and SGM               |
| OER (Company Grade)       | 2LT through CPT; WO1/CW2  |
| OER (Field Grade)         | MAJ, LTC; CW3 through CW5 |
| OER (Strategic / Colonel) | COL                       |

[See Army examples](/bullet-generator/examples/army).

### Navy EVAL, FITREP and Chief Eval sections

EVAL - Tailored covers NAVPERS 1616/26 for E1 through E6.
FITREP - Tailored covers NAVPERS 1610/2 for W2 through O6.
Both let you select the section you are writing.

Chief Eval Summary focuses on Block 40 Reporting Senior Comments on
Performance for NAVPERS 1616/27.

[See Navy examples](/bullet-generator/examples/navy).
